About

I'm a Computer Engineering graduate from the University of Washington Bothell, where I finished my BS in June 2025.

My hands-on development work is in Java, Python, and C/C++ — object-oriented system design, multithreaded programming, and test-driven verification. What I'm best at is debugging complex real-time systems that bridge hardware and software, from embedded control loops to distributed data systems.

I've designed custom PCBs and written firmware for vehicle control, built multithreaded systems on cloud infrastructure, and wired microcontrollers up to the internet. Most of it in agile, cross-disciplinary teams, in Git, across the full development lifecycle.

Lately I've been working on where AI fits into practical engineering workflows — which is what led to the recruiting assistant platform, and to building this site with an agentic coding setup.

Projects

Capstone

Drive-By-Wire for Electric Tricycles

Real-time C/C++ control of throttle, braking feedback, and automated steering for two electric tricycle platforms, running on custom PCBs I designed, populated, and debugged — routing power systems, wiring layouts, and sensor connections built to survive physical deployment.

Most of the work was on the bench and in the field: logging test hours operating the vehicles under real conditions, then tightening the steering logic against what actually happened rather than what the model predicted.
